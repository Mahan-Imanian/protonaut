# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- give and accept constructive criticism about the work, not the person;
- keep discussion on topic and useful for the people reading it later.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory
language or imagery, sexualised content, publishing someone's private
information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and temporarily or
permanently block participants who do not follow this code. Decisions
consider the impact on the people affected.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the contact on
the maintainer's GitHub profile. If the problem involves a maintainer,
report it to GitHub through the "Report content" option. Reports are kept
confidential.

This code is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
